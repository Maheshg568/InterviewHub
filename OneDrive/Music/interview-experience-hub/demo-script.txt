Interview Experience Hub - Demo Script (60–90 seconds)
=======================================================

**Preparation:**
Make sure backend and frontend are running (`npm run dev` in both, or `node server.js` + `npm run dev`).
Make sure `npm run seed` was executed in the backend folder to create the test accounts.

**The Pitch (15 seconds)**
"Welcome to Interview Experience Hub. This platform bridges the gap between students and industry experts, giving students real-world interview practice, structured feedback, and AI-assisted preparation before they face real job interviews."

---

**The Walkthrough (60 seconds)**

1. **Landing Page:**
   Show the clean, minimalist landing page with the hero background and call-to-action buttons.

2. **Register/Login:**
   "Let's log in as our demo student, Alice."
   Log in with `student@demo.com` / `password123`.

3. **Student Dashboard & Book:**
   Show the student dashboard. See the "Milestone" celebration banner for her past completed interview.
   Click "Book Slot" for Bob Interviewer (Google).
   Select "Frontend Development", choose a date and slot.
   Check the "Use Simulated Payment" box for the demo (bypasses file upload requirement).
   Submit. "The booking is now pending verification."

4. **Interviewer Accept:**
   Open a new tab (or incognito) and log in as `interviewer@demo.com` / `password123`.
   Show the Interviewer Dashboard stats.
   Find Alice's pending request. Note the payment is awaiting/simulated. 
   Paste a dummy Zoom link (e.g., https://zoom.us/j/123) and click "Accept & Confirm".

5. **Feedback:**
   "Once the interview ends, the interviewer provides structured feedback."
   Click "Complete Interview & Give Feedback".
   Fill out the scorecard (1-10) and type a short comment: "Alice did great on React hooks!"
   Submit Feedback.

6. **Progress View & AI Practice:**
   Go back to the Student window and refresh the dashboard.
   Scroll down to "Recent Feedback" and show the new feedback card with the scores and comment.
   Finally, click "AI Practice Module" at the top.
   Select "React Frontend" and click "Start Practice".
   Show how OpenAI generates 5 rapid-fire practice questions for Alice to prepare independently.

**Closing (5 seconds)**
"And that's how IEH empowers students with real experience, clear progress tracking, and AI-driven practice. Thank you!"
